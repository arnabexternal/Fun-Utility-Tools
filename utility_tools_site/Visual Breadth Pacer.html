Why visual breath?

Counting seconds in your head adds cognitive load. Here, the blob does the counting — you just follow its rhythm.

  • Cool cyan for inhale (expansive and airy).
  • Soft green-lavender for hold (stillness).
  • Warm peach for exhale (release).

Accessibility & safety

Audio cues mirror the visual motion so you can close your eyes and still stay in sync.

  • Subtle sine wave, not a harsh beep.
  • Fully adjustable volume & quick mute.
  • You stay in control — stop any time.
